1. Configure account rules

Edit reusable account profile fees

Default prices supplied by you: Standard is USD 49 evaluation plus USD 129 funded activation; No activation fee is USD 109 evaluation and USD 0 activation. Defaults apply to each supported platform. You can edit prices for the selected option; saving affects future setups. Existing setups and results retain their captured fees.

Leave a phase blank if its price is unknown. At least one fee is required. The No activation fee option always has zero activation. Defaults are saved separately for each platform and pricing option.

Evaluation and funded phases are independent starting modes. Fees are external cash expenses and do not reduce the trading balance.

Dates use YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ. Initial defaults span the selected source sessions, from first open to last reset.

Bar-close and trade-close observations are sampled views; neither establishes tick-level compliance. Historical evaluation is available; funded execution remains unavailable.

2. Declare instrument units

Confirm the saved monetary units and the instrument’s contract weight under the selected account rules. These cannot be inferred from its symbol.

3. Declare source coverage

Prop sessions use America/New_York: open 18:00 on the preceding date, close 16:59:59, reset 18:00. Sunday evening belongs to Monday. Coverage is a declaration about expected source bars, including days with no trades.

Declare every date in order, with status covered, closed, or missing. Covered days require UTC windows with inclusive first/last labels and a cadence. Missing coverage is rejected during the source check.

Explicit calendar format
[{"trading_date":"2026-01-05","status":"covered","windows":[{"first_utc":"2026-01-05T10:00:00Z","last_utc":"2026-01-05T10:30:00Z","bar_interval_seconds":300}]}]

Weekday approximation declares weekends closed and does not model holidays. Use explicit dates for exceptions. The full selected backtest period is checked; dates are not dropped to make coverage pass.
